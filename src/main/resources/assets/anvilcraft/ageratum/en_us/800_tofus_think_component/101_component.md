# Component

> A very vanilla-flavored entry system?

- Magnetic Attraction: Automatically absorb loot and experience gained from killing or mining.-> Using a handheld magnet on the right side of the anvil GUI can attach the entry to the item on the left. When placed above the grinding wheel and the lower slot is empty, the entry will be removed. As an additional feature, the experience gained will not be affected by the absorption cooldown.


## From[Reforge](010_reforge.md)
- Dense: Increases armor and protection performance by 10% -> When calculating the damage reduction for this section, an additional 10% of the pre-calculation damage is subtracted, but it cannot exceed half of the remaining damage. For example, if your armor achieves an 80% damage reduction this time, it will be adjusted to 90%. However, if any method already reaches 90%, it will be further adjusted to 95%
- Antidote: Reduces most negative effects by 80%, with a 50% chance to immunity(This is too long to write down: If the reduction is less than 2 seconds, it will be directly immune)
- Heavy Hit：add 50% Crit Damage
- Immortal: Immunity to the vast majority of damage, will not fade away over time when as ItemEntity
