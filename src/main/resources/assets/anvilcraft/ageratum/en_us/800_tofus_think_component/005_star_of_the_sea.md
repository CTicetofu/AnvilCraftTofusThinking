# Star Of The Sea

<item id="anvilcraft_tofus_thinking:star_of_the_sea"/>

> The Heart of the Sea can form a attackable conuit, while the Nether Star comes from a wither spirit with certain rebound ability. So, when combined together, it is of course an anti damage shield

- When used, it can be blocked like a shield in the picture, but the difference is that there is no directional restriction, which can be considered as forming a force field
- After using for more than 6 seconds, it will cool down, but under shield breaking attacks, it will only cool down for 3 seconds
- When lifting 5-20tick, it is considered a perfect block and can cause damage to attackers
- It can block magic damage related to the Vanilla. For damage that cannot be blocked by the shield, its damage value will be reduced by half
- Blocking certain damages may absorb the energy and use it to create new items, which will be mentioned in future items
